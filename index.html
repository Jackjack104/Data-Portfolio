Analytics approach

From operational question to usable decision.

  1. 01

    Define the decision

    Establish the business question, metric logic, and intended action before building the output.

  2. 02

    Validate the data

    Profile sources, document assumptions, and create checks around the logic that matters.

  3. 03

    Build for reuse

    Use models and workflows that can be trusted, maintained, and applied again.

  4. 04

    Make the conclusion clear

    Separate observed facts from unsupported explanations and make the next action obvious.
